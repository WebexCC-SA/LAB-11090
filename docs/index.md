# Getting Started with Webex Calling

**LAB-11090** is a four-hour, hands-on lab for configuring Webex Calling in Control Hub. Work through the nine activities to configure an organization, users, devices, calling features, call routing, and user settings for the VitaCrunch Foods scenario.

## Start Here

1. Review the [Overview and Objectives](lab-overview-objectives.md).
2. Read the [VitaCrunch Foods customer scenario](example-customer-scenario.md).
3. Open [Getting Started with Your Environment](getting-started-with-your-environment.md) and sign in with the credentials provided by your instructor.
4. Complete the labs in order and stop at each red STOP callout for instructor instructions.

## Lab Activities

Use the navigation to open Labs 1–9.

## Disclaimer

The lab configuration examples are for learning. For design questions, contact your Cisco representative or Cisco partner.
